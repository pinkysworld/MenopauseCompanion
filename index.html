Carefully scoped information

Menopause Companion offers educational content and self-tracking tools. It does not replace medical advice. If you have urgent or severe symptoms, contact your local emergency services or healthcare provider.

Questions about the app? Reach us at mip@gmx.biz.

Privacy you can feel good about

Your health data is sensitive. Menopause Companion is built to keep information on your device and only sync it if you explicitly turn on iCloud. We do not sell data or use advertising trackers.

On-device by default

Data stays local unless you opt in to iCloud sync.

Clear consent

HealthKit access only if you grant permission in iOS settings.

No tracking ads

The app does not include advertising SDKs.
