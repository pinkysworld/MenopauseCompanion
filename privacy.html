Privacy Policy

Your privacy, respected by design.

Menopause Companion is built to keep your information private and under your control. This policy explains what data is processed, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Effective date: January 24, 2026 Contact: mip@gmx.biz

Quick summary

  • No account required to use the app.
  • Data stays on your device unless you enable iCloud sync.
  • We do not sell personal data or run advertising trackers.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple, not by us.

Information you provide

Menopause Companion stores the data you enter, such as symptoms, mood, sleep, treatments, notes, and goals. This information is used to show your history, trends, and insights.

Health and wellness data

Entries you log in the app, including symptoms, mood, and lifestyle notes.

Preferences

Settings you choose, such as reminders, tracking options, and display preferences.

Support messages

When you contact support, we receive the details you send us by email.

HealthKit and optional data sources

If you grant permission, the app can read specific HealthKit data such as sleep metrics. HealthKit access is optional and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

How your data is stored

On-device by default

Your data is stored locally on your device. You can use the app without creating an account.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Apple stores encrypted copies in your iCloud account so your data is available across devices.

How we use your information

  • Provide in-app history, insights, and charts based on your entries.
  • Process support requests and respond to your questions.
  • Improve app stability and reliability based on feedback.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party ad networks.
  • We do not access HealthKit data without your explicit permission.

Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store payment card details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete entries inside the app at any time. You may also use the app's data delete option in Settings to remove local information.

Children's privacy

Menopause Companion is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when updates are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact mip@gmx.biz.
