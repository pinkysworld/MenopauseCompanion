Help topics by feature

Getting started & onboarding

Set up your profile and tracking preferences on first launch.

  1. Open the app and tap "Get Started".
  2. Read the healthcare disclaimer and toggle "I understand and agree".
  3. Select your current menopause stage.
  4. Choose your goals for tracking.
  5. Pick your tracking preferences and finish onboarding.
Daily check-in

A quick snapshot of sleep, mood, stress, hot flashes, and top symptoms.

  1. Open the Dashboard tab and tap the Daily Check-In card.
  2. Adjust Sleep, Mood, and Stress sliders.
  3. Choose your Hot Flash intensity.
  4. Select top symptoms and add notes (optional).
  5. Tap "Save Check-In".
Log symptom

Record symptom type, severity, duration, and optional tags.

  1. Go to Dashboard -> Quick Actions -> Log Symptom.
  2. Choose the date & time.
  3. Select the symptom type.
  4. Set severity and (optional) duration.
  5. Add tags or notes, then tap "Save Symptom".
Log sleep

Track sleep quality, hours slept, awakenings, and night sweats.

  1. Go to Dashboard -> Quick Actions -> Log Sleep.
  2. Set the sleep date.
  3. Adjust sleep quality and hours slept.
  4. Add awakenings and night sweats if applicable.
  5. Tap "Save Sleep Entry".
Log mood

Track mood, stress, and anxiety levels.

  1. Go to Dashboard -> Quick Actions -> Log Mood.
  2. Choose the entry date.
  3. Adjust mood, stress, and anxiety sliders.
  4. Add notes if you want context.
  5. Tap "Save Mood Entry".
Log trigger

Capture possible triggers like caffeine, alcohol, stress, or travel.

  1. Go to Dashboard -> Quick Actions -> Log Trigger.
  2. Set the date & time.
  3. Select the trigger type and intensity.
  4. Add notes if needed.
  5. Tap "Save Trigger".
History & filters

Review your past entries with filters and date ranges.

  1. Open the History tab.
  2. Use the Filter control to choose All, Check-Ins, Symptoms, Sleep, Mood, or Triggers.
  3. Use the Date Range menu to pick a timeframe.
  4. Scroll the list to review entries.
Insights

See patterns and explanations based on your tracking.

  1. Open the Insights tab.
  2. Tap the refresh icon to generate the latest insights.
  3. Scroll the insight cards and tap one to view details.
  4. Swipe down to close the detail view.
Advanced charts (Premium)

Visualize trends across longer time windows.

  1. In Insights, tap the chart icon in the top right.
  2. Select "Advanced Charts".
  3. Choose a time range (1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y).
  4. Scroll through the charts to review trends.
Treatment tracker (Premium)

Compare symptom trends before and after starting a treatment.

  1. In Insights, tap the chart icon -> Treatment Tracker.
  2. Select a treatment from the list to analyze it.
  3. Review the Before and After sections and the recommendation.
  4. If no treatments appear, add a treatment in your build (if available) or contact support.
Learn library

Read evidence-informed education content.

  1. Open the Learn tab.
  2. Browse the categories and select an article.
  3. Read the content and use Back to return.
  4. Premium lessons show a lock icon until upgraded.
Partner mode

Educational lessons to build empathy and support.

  1. Open the Partner tab.
  2. Toggle "Read Together Mode" for larger text (optional).
  3. Tap a lesson card to open it.
  4. If a lesson is premium, upgrade to unlock.
PDF exports (Premium)

Create shareable summaries for appointments.

  1. Open Settings -> Data & Privacy -> Export Data.
  2. Select "PDF Export (Premium)".
  3. Choose how you want to share or save the file.
App lock

Protect access with Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode.

  1. Open Settings -> Security -> App Lock.
  2. Toggle App Lock on.
  3. Authenticate when prompted.
  4. Toggle off any time to disable.
Apple Health import (Premium)

Import sleep data from Apple Health.

  1. Open Settings -> Health Data.
  2. Tap "Import Sleep Data".
  3. Approve Health access when iOS prompts you.
  4. Run the import again any time to update.
iCloud backup & sync (Premium)

Keep data synced across devices.

  1. Open Settings -> Backup & Sync.
  2. Turn on "iCloud Backup".
  3. If prompted, sign in to iCloud in iOS Settings.
  4. Tap "Sync Now" to run a manual sync.
Premium access

Unlock advanced insights, charts, and exports.

  1. Open Settings -> Subscription.
  2. Tap "Upgrade to Premium".
  3. Complete the purchase in the App Store.
  4. Return to unlock premium features.
Restore purchases

Re-check your Apple subscription on a new device.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap "Restore Purchases".
  3. Wait for the confirmation message.
Manage data

Export or delete your data.

  1. Open Settings -> Data & Privacy.
  2. Use "Export Data" to back up information.
  3. Tap "Delete All Data" to remove local data.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Tracking preferences

Customize what you track each day.

  1. Open Settings -> Tracking Preferences.
  2. Tap "Customize Tracking".
  3. Toggle on the items you want to track.
  4. Return to the Dashboard to see the updated flow.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Update to the latest iOS version and app build.
  • Restart the app before trying again.
  • For subscription issues, tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings.
  • Verify iCloud is enabled in device settings if sync is on.

Medical concerns

Menopause Companion does not provide medical advice. If you have severe or urgent symptoms, contact your healthcare provider or local emergency services.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple will re-check your subscription and unlock premium if it is active.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored on your device. iCloud sync is optional and only occurs if you enable it in-app and in iOS settings.

Does the app share my information?

We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising SDKs. See the privacy policy for full details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete entries in the app. For full removal, use the in-app delete option in Settings, which clears local data.

How do I enable HealthKit sleep import?

In Settings, turn on HealthKit access and allow sleep data when iOS prompts you. You can change permissions any time in the Health app.

What is Partner Mode?

Partner Mode is a set of educational lessons designed to help loved ones understand and support someone experiencing menopause.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Premium users can generate PDF summaries to share with clinicians or keep for personal records.

How does iCloud sync work?

If enabled, iCloud sync stores your data in your iCloud account so it appears on all signed-in devices. It can be turned off any time in Settings.
